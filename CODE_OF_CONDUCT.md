# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Wollipog community respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- communicate with empathy and respect;
- give and accept constructive technical feedback;
- focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than people;
- respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience;
- take responsibility for mistakes and work toward repair.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, community spaces, and public interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the privacy and safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through an available private channel. Do not publish sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers will acknowledge reports as promptly as practical and will avoid conflicts of interest when selecting who handles a report.
